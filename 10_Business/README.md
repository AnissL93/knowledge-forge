# Business

This folder holds *reusable business knowledge*, not all startup work.

## Company
One note per company:
- competitors
- potential partners
- customer companies
- companies worth tracking long-term

## Market
Market / industry research organized around a question:
- market size
- user needs
- pricing
- channels
- industry structure
- competitive landscape

## Customers
User interviews, ICP, personas, customer insights.

## Strategy
Relatively stable strategic judgments:
- Positioning
- GTM
- Pricing strategy
- Moat
- Business model

## One important rule

The *thing you are pushing forward* goes in `01_Projects`.

For example:

- `01_Projects/Launch MVP.md`
- `01_Projects/Competitor landscape Q4.md`
- `01_Projects/Customer discovery - Design agencies.md`

The reusable knowledge a project produces settles into `10_Business`.

For example:

- `10_Business/Company/Competitor A.md`
- `10_Business/Market/AI design tools.md`
- `10_Business/Customers/Design agency ICP.md`
