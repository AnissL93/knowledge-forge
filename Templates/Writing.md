-  Every major factual claim has a source
    
-  Dates and numbers verified

-  Counterarguments represented fairly
    
-  Distinction between fact and my interpretation is clear
    

# Editing Passes

## Structure

-  Thesis is clear
    
-  Sections follow logically
    
-  No unnecessary sections
    
-  Conclusion answers the opening question
    

## Clarity

-  Each paragraph has one main idea
    
-  Technical terms are explained
    
-  Long sentences simplified
    
-  Repetition removed
    

## Evidence

-  Claims have evidence
    
-  Weak evidence identified
    
-  Important uncertainty stated
    

## Style

-  Opening is strong
    
-  Transitions are natural
    
-  Tone fits audience
    
-  Jargon reduced where possible
